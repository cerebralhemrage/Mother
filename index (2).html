— for Jeanne —

A Poem for You

Before I knew the names of things,
you taught me what was gentle—
the steady hand, the quiet song,
the love that was not rental.
You held the world together, Jeanne,
with tea cups and with patience,
with open doors and open arms
and grace in every cadence.
The years have turned like garden soil,
and still your roots run under
each good and growing thing I am—
you are my oldest wonder.
So here's a truth I'll set in ink
no distance can undo:
the best of what I'll ever be
began its life in you.

— with all my love

To the Woman Who Made It All Possible

Thank you for every unseen sacrifice, every quiet prayer, and every moment you chose love when the world gave you every reason not to. You are more extraordinary than you know, Mom. Today and every day, I celebrate you.

With love, always.
